# 2026-07-27 — v1.1.1 release cut: a patch that was supposed to need a major

## What got done

- **`master` went green first.** The BUG-0001 fix merged as `dd452a7` and its CI run came back
  `completed/success` with **0 failing jobs** — `race` and `quality` included. First clean master
  since 2026-07-26, and the first execution of those assertions under the real detector.
  *(The monitor I armed to watch it was broken: it shelled out to standalone `jq`, which is not
  installed here — every earlier use had been `gh --jq`, gh's built-in. Forty iterations of silence
  that looked like waiting. Checked directly instead.)*

- **The maintainer asked whether v1.2.0 was due; it was not, and the reason is structural.** MINOR
  needs additive API, and there is none pending — ADR-0030's bucket 1 was the entire additive
  backlog and Milestone 10 consumed all thirteen items into v1.1.0. Bucket 2 is breaking by
  construction, bucket 3 is deviations kept. A v1.2.0 needs capability nobody has planned yet.

- **But a v1.1.1 was sitting there, and it overturns something we had been carrying since 10.10.**
  The `middleware.HeaderName` allocation cost has been recorded in three documents as *"measured but
  not changed, since it is an API-visible constant under the v1 commitment"* — deferred to a `/v2`
  that ADR-0041 and ADR-0042 both call unscheduled. **That conflated two different things.** The
  two allocations come from passing a non-canonical key to `Header.Get`/`Set`, which makes
  `textproto.CanonicalMIMEHeaderKey` allocate. The *value* of the exported constant and the *cost of
  using it as a map key* are separable, and only the first is a compatibility question.
- **So: an unexported `canonicalHeaderName = "X-Request-Id"` for map access, `HeaderName` untouched.**
  Nothing observable changed — `Header.Set` already canonicalised before storing, so the map and the
  wire have been `X-Request-Id` all along. Measured, hardware-independent: `RequestID` **6 → 4**
  allocs, `Chain` **8 → 6**, `ChainWithLogger` **11 → 9**, `RequestIDGenerated` **7 → 5**.
  [ADR-0044](../../../adr/0044-canonical-header-key-for-map-access.md).
- **The ratchet was lowered and proved to bind.** `allocBudget` goes to RequestID 4, Chain 6,
  ChainWithLogger 9; dropping RequestID to 3 fails the gate. A ratchet left slack after an
  improvement silently re-admits the regression it just removed.
- **The latency gain is reported, not claimed.** ~675 ns for the non-logging chain against 938 ns,
  consistent with two fewer allocations — but `ChainWithLogger` ranged 3.2–15.7 µs across three runs
  on this box. ADR-0037's own methodology gates the hardware-independent numbers and reports the
  rest; the allocation counts carry this.
- **Three documents that said "blocked behind a major" were corrected** — ROADMAP's M10 preamble,
  ADR-0037's deferred list *and* its rejected-alternative paragraph (annotated as "correct
  conclusion, faulty middle step" rather than rewritten), and the NFR report, updated in place per
  the 10.11 precedent so before/after sits in one document. The v1.1.0 release notes keep their
  original wording: they describe what shipped then and are not retconned.
- **Release cut per `release.md` steps 1–5:** `version.go` → 1.1.1, README badge, `[Unreleased]`
  *moved* into `docs/changelog/v1/v1.1.1.md` leaving the empty skeleton plus an index row,
  `docs/releases/v1.1.1.md` written and indexed, and **BUG-0001's `fixed-in` finally resolved from
  the placeholder `unreleased` to `v1.1.1`**. The lockstep gate was verified live rather than
  trusted: reverting `version.go` to 1.1.0 fails it on all three edges.


findings *recorded correctly and then inherited without re-reading*. BUG-0001's alloc assertions had

conclusion stops being questioned once it is written down, which is the same failure the M11/M12
